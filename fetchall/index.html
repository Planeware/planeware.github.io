Privacy Policy — FetchAll

By Plainware · Effective: October 2026

FetchAll is built on a simple principle: your browsing stays yours. The extension runs entirely on your device. We operate no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

What FetchAll does with your data

  • Page scanning happens locally. When you click the FetchAll icon, the extension scans the page you are viewing for file links. This happens in your browser's memory, on your device. Page contents are never sent anywhere.
  • Downloads use Chrome's built-in download manager. Files are saved directly to your computer in the location you choose in Chrome's settings. We never see, store, or proxy your files.
  • Settings stay in your browser. Preferences (filters, rename masks, license status) are stored with Chrome's local storage APIs on your device.

What we do NOT collect

  • No browsing history or page contents
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking pixels
  • No personal information — there is no account to create

Pro licenses (Lemon Squeezy)

If you purchase FetchAll Pro, license activation and validation are handled by our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy (a Stripe company and merchant of record). When you enter a license key, the extension contacts api.lemonsqueezy.com to verify it. The data sent is limited to the license key you entered and a random installation identifier generated on your device. Lemon Squeezy's handling of payment information is governed by Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy. We receive no payment details.

Permissions explained

  • activeTab — lets FetchAll scan the page you click it on, and only that page.
  • scripting — runs the page scan that finds file links.
  • downloads — saves the files you choose to your computer.
  • storage — remembers your settings and license status locally.
  • offscreen — keeps large download batches running reliably in the background.
  • Optional host permissions (Pro only) — for multi-tab batches and ZIP packaging, the extension asks your permission per site, at the moment you use the feature. Nothing is granted at install time, and free users are never asked.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because we do not collect any. The only third party the extension ever communicates with is Lemon Squeezy, solely for Pro license verification as described above.

Children

FetchAll is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them (we do not knowingly collect information from anyone).
